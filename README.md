- **Who it serves:** retailers looking for visible shops or showrooms; growing businesses needing offices; logistics and industrial operators seeking warehouses; and property owners hoping to lease commercial space.

## The problem

Commercial leasing decisions are driven by suitability, not just attractive property photographs. A retail business may prioritise road frontage and customer footfall; a growing company may need the right office size, parking and lift access; a warehouse tenant may focus on loading space, truck access and power supply.

A single unstructured property gallery makes these requirements difficult to compare. NMB therefore needs a website where **different property types have recognisable search journeys**, individual listings explain practical specifications, and enquiries reach the leasing team with enough detail to begin a meaningful conversation.

The owner side of the marketplace creates a second challenge: commercial property owners must be able to share a property, its dimensions, facilities and asking rent without dealing with a complicated sign-up process.

No independently documented previous-site audit was available, so the challenges above describe the business needs evidenced by the live platform, not verified shortcomings of an earlier version.

## How Tech Conversion presented the project

The following describes **publicly observable website features and the published portfolio direction**. It should not be read as a detailed internal build log or a claim that every security, performance or integration test was completed.

1. **Separate property searches by business need.** The main navigation routes visitors into [Retail Spaces](https://nmbleasingsolutions.com/retail-spaces/), [Office Spaces](https://nmbleasingsolutions.com/office-spaces/), [Warehouses](https://nmbleasingsolutions.com/warehouses/) and [Commercial Land](https://nmbleasingsolutions.com/commercial-land/).
2. **Provide meaningful filters.** Listing pages expose location, size, budget and category-related filtering, helping a visitor move from broad browsing to more relevant options.
3. **Make individual properties useful to evaluate.** Public listings present combinations of floor area, monthly rent, floor position, frontage, utilities and other property details, with contact options for follow-up.
4. **Create a dedicated owner-listing journey.** The [List Your Property](https://nmbleasingsolutions.com/list-your-property/) form collects owner and property information, rent expectations, amenities, images and optional floor plans. It also offers a WhatsApp route.
5. **Ask tenants about their actual requirements.** The [Contact](https://nmbleasingsolutions.com/contact-us/) page includes property category, preferred locality, required area, budget, timing and other enquiry fields so the leasing team can shortlist suitable spaces.
6. **Explain the leasing process.** The homepage sets out a clear progression from requirement sharing to shortlisted options, site visits, negotiations, documentation and move-in support.
7. **Show local market coverage.** The homepage presents commercial hubs across Andhra Pradesh, giving visitors context beyond a single property or neighbourhood.
8. **Keep direct contact visible.** Calls, WhatsApp and enquiry actions appear throughout the public site to help turn property research into a conversation.

## Standards the website should be reviewed against

The following are **evaluation references**, not evidence of completed certification or auditing.

- [Core Web Vitals](https://web.dev/articles/vitals): assess mobile loading, interactivity and layout stability across results, image galleries and detail pages.
- [WCAG 2.2, Level AA](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/): check accessible search filters, keyboard operation, form labels, error messaging and image alternatives.
- [Google Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials): review crawlable property categories, useful location content and accurate listing information.
- [Schema.org](https://schema.org/): evaluate relevant organisation, breadcrumb and property-listing markup when supported by valid, current information.
- [India's Digital Personal Data Protection framework](https://www.meity.gov.in/data-protection-framework): review consent, retention and sharing practices for owner details, prospective tenants and uploaded property documentation.
- **Listing accuracy:** confirm advertised availability, floor areas, rent, ownership-related claims and other decision-critical information with the leasing team before publication.
